(`scripts/verify-dogfood.sh --update`). Do not hand-edit.

What the ratio counts: `bound` is the members the plugin's own `bridgeable*`
filters keep, and `public` is an independent count the metadata reader takes off
the assembly BEFORE its top-level-only type filter and before any bridgeability
decision. So the denominator includes members this project drops with no
diagnostic at all: the members of nested public types, the members of a struct
that fails ADR-056, and every operator. Those are real gaps and the ratio is
meant to be held against them.

What the ratio does NOT count: protected and internal members (out of scope),
inherited members (the RIR carries no base type, so a derived wrapper never
gets them and they are in neither half), and anything about CORRECTNESS.
`generation: ok` means both generators returned without throwing. It does not
mean the generated Kotlin or C# compiles; nothing here compiles anything.
